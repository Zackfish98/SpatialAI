Home Depot Robot

Rules
  1. Robot picks ordered items (max 3), then goes to the loading-zone waiting area.
  2. It avoids shelves, walls and cars, and re-plans around people. A person ahead within 2.6 m makes it slow down (amber flashing light); within 1 m it stops (red flashing light + “PLEASE MOVE”). If no clear path exists it creeps through any gap, or waits until people move.
  3. The app registers the client's plate + car type and closes the loading zone to everyone but the client while a car is present; shoppers are kept out or asked to leave.
  4. It waits until the client's car is parked at the assigned spot and the client confirms their location.
  5. The app-listed car type is only a guess; the type confirmed on site decides how to load.
  6. Loading type sets approach gap, lift height and max cargo length (Pickup 3.0 m, SUV 2.5 m, Sedan trunk 1.3 m).
  7. If cargo won't fit the confirmed vehicle, the robot stops and requests staff assist.
