Neighborhood

Rules
  1. One thing per cell.
  2. Houses, Shops and Towers must touch a road (edge-adjacent). Parks may go anywhere.
  3. A road can't be removed if it would cut off a building.
  4. House = 4 residents/level, Tower = 10/level. Levels 1–3.
  5. Level 2+ needs a Shop within 3 cells; level 3 also needs a Park within 3.
  6. A Shop serves 16 residents within 3 cells; unserved residents are unhappy.
  7. No two Shops may be adjacent.
